PouchPath is a local-first nicotine-pouch tracker and quit-plan companion for iPhone and Apple Watch. It helps you build space between pouches, understand patterns, and follow an awareness, taper, or quit-date plan without turning a daily ceiling into a goal to finish.

## Get help

- [Report a problem or request help](https://github.com/tylerbrevard/pouchpath-support/issues/new)
- [Read the privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})

Please do not post pouch history, craving notes, supporter details, exports, or other sensitive personal information in a public issue.

### Before reporting a sync problem

1. Open **More → Privacy & Data** and note the sync status.
2. Confirm you are signed into iCloud and have network access.
3. Export a restorable backup before reinstalling or erasing anything.

PouchPath is for education and self-management, not medical advice. Nicotine is addictive. For immediate medical danger, call local emergency services. In the United States, Poison Help is **1-800-222-1222** and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available by calling or texting **988**.
