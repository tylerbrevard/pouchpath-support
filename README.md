# PouchPath Support

PouchPath is a local-first nicotine-pouch tracker and quit-planning app for iPhone, with optional Widget, Apple Watch quick logging, device backup, and private CloudKit sync.

For help or a bug report, use the repository's **Issues** tab. Do not post pouch history, craving notes, supporter details, exports, or other sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Before reporting a sync problem:

1. Open **More → Privacy & Data** and note the sync status.
2. Confirm you are signed into iCloud and have network access.
3. Export a restorable backup before reinstalling or erasing anything.

PouchPath is for education and self-management, not medical advice. For immediate medical danger call local emergency services. In the United States, Poison Help is 1-800-222-1222 and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available by calling or texting 988.
